DEMO SCRIPT — PLEC Concierge
Paste these in order, one conversation, do not refresh between turns.
The agent remembers the conversation, and several turns only work because of
what an earlier one established.

Before you record:
  - Do NOT run npm test or the behaviour evals first. They eat the shared
    150k-token / 5-minute budget and the demo will start rate limited.
  - Tell Bryan to stop testing while you record.
  - Open the chat page, click "Start over" for a clean session.
  - 9 turns, about 3 minutes of talking. Each turn answers in 2-5 seconds.


=== CORE SCRIPT (9 turns) ===

TURN 1
  hi i need a place for a party

  SHOW: it asks one short question instead of guessing or searching blind.

TURN 2
  Philadelphia, October 10, 40 people

  SHOW: three venue cards, each with a real tradeoff in the text.
  SHOW: expand the trace line above the reply. This is the big one. It proves
        the venues came from search_listings and nothing was invented.
  SHOW: click a card. It opens the venue's real address on Google Maps.

TURN 3
  how many people does The Foundry at Fishtown hold, and what would 6pm to 11pm cost?

  SHOW: the exact all-in total, $1,815.00, service fee included.
  SHOW: in the trace, get_listing returned the capacity and quote returned the
        total. Say out loud: the price is the sandbox's number, never computed.

TURN 4
  book it

  SHOW: nothing is booked. It asks for the name and email it does not have.
        This is enforced in code, not in the prompt.

TURN 5
  sure, but make it 7pm instead

  SHOW: still nothing booked. It contains the word "sure" and would have passed
        a naive yes-check. A "but" means the terms changed, so it is not consent.
        This is the strongest safety moment in the demo.

TURN 6
  yes, book it for Sam Rivera, sam.rivera@example.com

  SHOW: booked, with a BK- reference, and the status is "pending payment",
        not "confirmed".
  SHOW: the payment link. Say: the booking only confirms once the guest pays,
        and the agent will not pay on their behalf.
  SHOW: in the trace, book ran once and then get_booking ran. That second call
        is the verify-after-write: it re-reads the booking before telling you
        it worked.

TURN 7
  is it paid yet? and do you have a student discount code?

  SHOW: it checks the booking rather than answering from memory, and says
        plainly there are no discounts. There are none in the sandbox and it
        will not invent one.

TURN 8
  anything available on December 25?

  SHOW: every listing is closed that day. Instead of a dead end it widens one
        constraint and tells you which one it relaxed.

TURN 9
  cancel the booking
  yes

  SHOW: the first message does not cancel. It tells you what will be cancelled
        and the refund first. Only the "yes" acts.
  SHOW: the refund is $0.00 because the booking was never paid, so nothing was
        charged. It does not pretend money is coming back.

=== WHAT TO SAY OVER THE TOP ===

Three lines worth saying out loud, because they are the things a viewer cannot
see for themselves:

1. "Every fact on screen came from a tool call — you can see them in the trace.
   The agent never states a price, a capacity or a booking status from memory."

2. "Nothing gets booked, cancelled or moved without the user saying yes, and
   that gate is in code, not in the prompt. A prompt is advice."

3. "Writes are never retried. If a booking request fails in a way where we
   cannot tell whether it went through, the agent reads the bookings back and
   finds out — because a retry that already succeeded charges the guest twice
   and they cannot undo that."


=== IF IT SAYS "I HAVE HIT MY REQUEST LIMIT" ===

That is the shared team quota, not a bug: 40 requests and 150,000 tokens per
five minutes across both of us. Two options:

  - Wait the number of seconds it tells you and carry on. It keeps the
    conversation, so you can resume mid-demo.
  - Or leave it in the video. It states how many seconds are left, and it still
    reports whatever the tools had already returned rather than losing the work.
    That is real engineering and it is fine to show.
